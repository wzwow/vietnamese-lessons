Vietnamese learning plan

60 lessons · 6 phases of 10 · every 5th lesson is a review (warm-up + quiz).

Progress: 3 / 60 lessons
Done: lessons 1–3 (as of 28 Oct 2026) · Next up: Lesson 4: Pronouns (tôi, bạn, anh, chị, em)

How the plan works

Each lesson page: ① warm-up review of earlier phrases ② 4–5 new phrases with neural audio (normal + 🐢 slow, tap words) ③ one grammar point ④ mini dialogue ⑤ quiz ⑥ speak-back with 🎤. Review lessons (5, 10, 15 …) show only the warm-up and quiz. Warm-ups recycle phrases from 1, 3, 7 and 14 lessons back.
